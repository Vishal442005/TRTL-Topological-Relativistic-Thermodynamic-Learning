\textbf{Background and Motivation:} \\
For more than six decades, foundational classification paradigms---ranging from classical hyperplanes (Kernel SVMs) and orthogonal step partitions (Random Forests, XGBoost) to piecewise-affine deep neural networks (MLPs, Transformers)---have partitioned feature space via global, ungrounded decision boundaries.
